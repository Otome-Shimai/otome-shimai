
# The Prince of Tennis Sweet School Festival \~♡-40 and more...~ General Gameplay Guide

## Starting a new game

- Choose “New Game” on the home screen
- Select which school you will be from - your Love Interest for this playthrough will only be able to be from the school you attend!
- Enter your first and last names, you will go by the last name you choose for most of the game. LIs you have high affection with will start calling you by your first name after a while.
- The game will throw you into the story!
## Your Room

At the beginning and end of each day your bedroom acts as a Menu where you can Save/Load or Check Affection among other options. Once you are done with the day in the evening, select “Next Day” to move on.

## Map Options

You will be able to “Look” multiple times and “Chat” 1 to 3 times per time period every day, so you’ll need to speak to more guys than just the LI you are going for. 

I also recommend moving your cursor over the blank areas of the map, you might find something fun!
## Affection Levels

Your affection will go up or down during interactions with the LIs, their love meter is a giant heart at the top right of the screen. The giant heart will fill up slightly more every time you fill up the smaller hearts below it. You can also check everyone’s affection levels by choosing “Check Affection” in the Menu.
## Conversation Topics

When you “Chat” with an LI you typically get to choose the Topic, you’ll start out with basic Topics to choose from such as “Homework” or “Friends”. Be warned, not every topic will raise affection! You will have many chances to raise affection levels, so it’s typically ok if you accidentally lose affection once or twice.

You will also occasionally get “Special Topics” which are LI specific. Make sure you use the “Special Topics” with the correct LI!
## Morning Greetings

You have a random chance of running into one of the LIs in the morning, each character has a different greeting they like that will raise their affection. You can also…touch them? I refer to it as “booping” them.

Once their affection is high enough they will greet you on their own.
## Walking Home

You will run into different LIs in the evening and have the option to ask them to walk to the station together, as their affection rises they may ask you to walk with them. Say yes to the one you are going for! You may also get the chance to “Touch” them, like during morning greetings.

Note: If you have high affection with more than one LI, you may have two try to walk home with you! Make sure you say NO to anyone you aren’t aiming for. If your chosen LI sees you walking with another guy they will lose affection!

## Phone Numbers  

Once your affection is high enough with an LI they will ask for your phone number, your safest bet is to say yes to the LI you are chasing, and no to anyone else. There are some LIs who won’t be romanceable anymore if you give your number to another guy!

## Jealousy

Some of the LIs will get JEALOUS if you speak to other guys on the same map. Before you talk to someone who isn’t your capture target, hover over their icon on the map and see if your target LI’s icon looks upset. If you speak to the other LI anyways, your capture target’s affection will go down! Some LIs get more jealous than others, so be careful!

Note: There may be situations where you are forced to speak to other LIs, it’s not the end of the world if your target’s affection goes down a bit as you will have chances to raise it again, but you should try to avoid it as much as possible.

## Dates

Once your target LI has your phone number, and if affection is high enough, they will call you the night before a rest day to ask you on a date! Say yes if it’s your capture target calling. The dates are fun!

## Mini Games

There are multiple mini games that will be unlocked as you play through the routes, instructions will be shown before you play the game. Please note you can’t play mini games with a mouse! You’ll need a controller.
