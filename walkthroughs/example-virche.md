Our guide for **Virche Evermore: -ErroR:Salvation-**, the beautifully bleak tale of
Ceres and the country of Arpechele.

## Before You Start

- Bring tissues. Seriously.
- The game is *very* linear about unlock order — follow the sequence below.
- Skip-read is your friend on repeat chapters (`Ctrl` to fast-skip).

## Recommended Route Order

1. Ceres's common chapters
2. Mathis
3. Yves
4. Scien
5. Lucas
6. Ankou
7. Le Salut (final route)

## Route Guide: Mathis (Example)

### Deathes and Salvations

Each love interest has both a **Salvation** ending and a **Deathe** ending — you need
*both* to unlock the finale.

- Salvation: pick the trusting/hopeful choices throughout.
- Deathe: pick the despairing choices from chapter 3 onward.

### Key Choices

| Scene | For Salvation | For Deathe |
|-------|---------------|------------|
| The workshop | **"I want to understand you"** | "This is hopeless" |
| The bridge at night | **Reach out** | Turn away |
| Final confrontation | **"Live, no matter what"** | Stay silent |
